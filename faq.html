Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register?

Open the Login page, choose Register, and fill out the form. Your account is saved locally in this browser.

Can I post updates?

Yes. After logging in, use the Social Feed page to create posts and add them to the feed.

Is data saved?

Your account and feed posts are stored in browser local storage until your browser data is cleared.

What pages are available?

Use Home, About, FAQ, Feed, Login, Dashboard, Account, Settings, Videos, Chat, Companies, Inventor, and Admin pages in this demo.
